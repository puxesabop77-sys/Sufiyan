About The Founder

Talat Yasmin is the visionary founder of Anti Lodge, a student welfare initiative established with the mission of providing a safe, affordable and supportive environment for students pursuing their education away from home.

Her journey reflects determination, resilience and an unwavering commitment to empowering young minds. Through her leadership, Anti Lodge has become more than just a place to stay—it has become a community where students can focus on learning, personal growth and success.

A True Example of Women Empowerment

Talat Yasmin stands as an inspiring example of women empowerment. Her achievements demonstrate that leadership, innovation and social impact know no boundaries. She has shown how vision, courage and dedication can transform an idea into an institution that positively influences countless lives.

Her story encourages young women to dream boldly, pursue excellence and contribute meaningfully to society through leadership and service.

Legacy and Impact

Under her guidance, Anti Lodge continues to evolve into a modern, technology-driven and student-focused organization. Her commitment to quality, discipline and welfare remains the foundation of everything the institution represents today.

🏆 Talat Yasmin's contribution extends beyond administration and management. She represents hope, empowerment, progress and the belief that strong leadership can transform communities and create opportunities for future generations.
